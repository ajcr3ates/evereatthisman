History

Nobody remembers cooking him. Yet every morning, somewhere in the world, a person looks down at breakfast and he looks back up at them.

The first recorded sighting came from a small café kitchen. A cook stirred a pot of porridge and found a face settling into the top of it. She showed the regulars, took a photo, and served it anyway. The customer said it was the best bowl he had ever eaten and that he felt watched until lunchtime.

Within weeks, similar reports arrived from people who had never met. At first the face appeared in oatmeal, granola bars, and other foods containing oats. We didn’t see it as an issue, until we’ve recently received reports of him appearing in other breakfast items, including toast, pancakes, eggs, and even latte art.

The purpose of this website is to gather more information about this phenomenon, and hopefully stop him before he infiltrates more foods and beverages.


Timeline of Sightings

  1. The first bowl. A café cook photographs the face in a pot of oatmeal. The picture gets passed around the regulars and later posted online.
  2. Granola bars. People started noticing that he was appearing in their granola bars. Gym goers reported that after eating the granola bar, they had the best workout of their lives.
  3. Other breakfasts. He began to spread and infiltrate a variety of breakfast foods including toast, eggs, and pancakes. Reporters say they felt uncomfortable.
  4. Latte art. Baristas report pouring him by accident. Several say they cannot pour anything else for the rest of the shift.
  5. This website. Ever Eat This Man? opens to collect the sightings and ask the obvious question: who is he, and why is he here?

Theories

Pareidolia. Your brain finds faces in any pattern of light and dark, and breakfast is full of them. This explains a face. It does not explain why it is always the same one.

The collective breakfast. Some visitors think he is a shared idea, passed from bowl to bowl the way a tune gets stuck in many heads at once.

Aliens. Some believe that he is a form of super extraterrestrial intelligence that humanity is unable to communicate with or understand.

He wants to be eaten. The most popular theory on the board, and the least scientific.
